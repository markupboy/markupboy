# Privacy Policy

_Last updated October 11, 2026_

Markupboy.com is the personal site of Blake Walters. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit, and what happens to it.

## Information I collect

I don't ask for personal information to browse this site. There are no accounts, no comment forms, and no newsletter signups.

Like most websites, the servers that host this site may automatically log basic technical details about each request, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time of the visit. These logs are used only to keep the site running and secure.

If you email me, I'll have your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

## Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't set cookies, and it doesn't use analytics or advertising trackers.

## Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, and it's served by a third-party hosting provider. Those services receive your IP address and browser details as part of delivering the page, and handle that information under their own privacy policies.

This site also links to other websites. I'm not responsible for the content or privacy practices of sites I don't run.

## How information is used

Anything collected is used to operate and maintain the site and to reply to people who get in touch. I don't sell or rent personal information, and I don't share it except where required by law.

## Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Changes

I may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date above.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to [FromTheWeb@markupboy.com](mailto:fromtheweb@markupboy.com).
